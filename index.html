Who we are

A house of encounter,
a family of fire

What began in 2014 as a small prayer group in Lubumbashi, DR Congo, has grown into an international ministry — with locations in Lubumbashi, Kinshasa, Zambia, Johannesburg and Bellevue, USA.

Founded by Prophet Daniel Ngoy — a charismatic prophet of God with a deep insight on the person and ministry of the Holy Spirit — HGIM exists to empower individuals to serve God in their unique callings and missions. Alongside him, Pastor Merveil Kalala oversees the spiritual growth of the congregation with a passion for teaching and discipleship.
