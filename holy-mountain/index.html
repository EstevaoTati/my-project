Who we are

A missionary community, planted in Washington, sent to the nations.

Holy Mountain Washington Church is the Washington congregation of the Communauté Évangélique Missionnaire La Montagne Sainte (CEMMS). We are a community of believers from many countries and languages, holding one confession: Jesus Christ is Lord.

We hold to the Bible as the Word of God, to salvation by grace through faith, to the work of the Holy Spirit in the life of the believer, and to the Great Commission as the assignment of every disciple — not only of pastors.

Vision & mission

Two sentences that decide everything we do.

Vision

Go out into the world

We do not exist for ourselves. The church is not the building we meet in — it is the people God sends out of it, into workplaces, campuses, neighbourhoods and nations, carrying the light they received.

Isaiah 43

Mission

Make disciples of Jesus

Not crowds — disciples. People who know the Word, walk in the Spirit, are formed in character, and are able in turn to lead someone else to Christ and teach them to obey everything he commanded.

Matthew 28:19

Ministries

There is a place here for what God put in you.

Every ministry is open to members and to guests who want to serve. Tell us where you would like to start and we will connect you with the person who leads it.

Worship & music

Singers, musicians and sound technicians who carry the congregation into the presence of God week after week.

Prayer & intercession

The engine room of the church. We pray for our city, our families, the sick and the nations, in person and on the phone.

Youth & children

Teaching that takes young people seriously, so the next generation owns the faith rather than inherits a habit.

Missions & evangelism

Outreach in the Washington area and support for the wider CEMMS missionary work — the reason the church carries the name it does.

Women & men fellowship

Smaller circles where marriage, work, money, singleness and faith are discussed honestly and prayed over.

Welcome & care

Hospitality, practical help for new arrivals in the United States, visits to the sick and support in bereavement.
